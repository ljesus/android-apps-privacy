ReelVault — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-21_

This policy explains, in plain English, what data the ReelVault Android app

(the "App") handles and what it never touches. The short version: the App

itself collects nothing about you. It is a viewer for a public film library,

and the only company involved besides us is Google, whose ad SDK keeps the

App free.

What the App itself collects

Nothing. The App has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics of its

own. Everything it stores stays in the App's local storage on your device:

  • the film catalog pages you have opened (cached so shelves open fast and

still work when your connection drops),

  • your last 20 search queries (kept on-device only, so search works without

an account),

  • your Data saver on/off setting,
  • small poster thumbnails from a capped disk cache (about 6 MB), and
  • ad-related timing values used to space out interstitial ads.

This data never leaves your device, is never sent to us or anyone else, is

not backed up to any cloud service by us, and is removed when you uninstall

the App — or at once via Settings → Catalog cache → Clear cache.

What leaves your phone, and why

ReelVault is a viewer for the Internet Archive's public-domain film

collections (archive.org). The App ships no video and hosts nothing —

every film page, poster and stream is fetched live from archive.org via its

public interfaces, and every film links back to its archive.org source page.

When you use the App:

  • your search words are sent to archive.org over HTTPS so the archive

can return results — exactly as if you had typed them into archive.org's

own search box,

  • the identifier of the film you open is sent to archive.org to fetch

its details and stream, and

  • video streams and poster images are downloaded **directly from

archive.org's servers** to your device.

Our code attaches nothing else to these requests: no account (there are

none), no name, no email, no phone number, no precise location, no

advertising identifier. The App sends only a standard version string

identifying itself ("ReelVault/1.0"). Archive.org is an independent

third-party site; its own privacy practices apply there, and we link every

film back to it. ReelVault is not affiliated with the Internet Archive.

Advertising

ReelVault is free and paid for by ads. To show ads, the App embeds the

Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Where required, Google's User Messaging

Platform shows a consent form first; your choice controls whether ads are

personalized.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use data as described in Google's

own policies, including:

  • the Advertising ID (and related device identifiers) of your device,
  • limited device and connection information (e.g. network state), and
  • diagnostics (e.g. that an ad request failed or succeeded).

Its standard purposes are to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud and

abuse. These identifiers and signals are handled by Google, not by us: we do

not receive them, do not build profiles from them, and cannot identify you

with them. Google's practices are described at

ads policies.

No third party other than Google (for advertising) and archive.org (to serve

the catalog, streams and posters you request) receives any data through the

App.

Data retention

  • Your App data (catalog cache, recent searches, settings, poster

thumbnails): kept on your device only, until you clear the cache in

Settings or uninstall the App.

  • Advertising-related data held by Google (e.g. the Advertising ID and

ad interaction records): governed by Google's policies and retention

terms.

Your controls

  • Ad personalization and the Advertising ID: manage or reset it in

Google's ads settings at

adssettings.google.com — you can turn ad

personalization off or reset the identifier at any time.

  • Consent: the consent form Google shows inside the App controls whether

you see personalized ads.

  • App data: Settings → Catalog cache → Clear cache removes cached

catalog pages, recent searches and poster thumbnails immediately.

  • Everything: uninstalling ReelVault removes all local App data.

Children

ReelVault is a general-audience film browser and is not directed at children

under 13, and Google's ad SDK is configured accordingly. We do not

knowingly collect any data from anyone.
