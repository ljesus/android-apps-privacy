BattMoji Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

This policy explains how BattMoji (the Android app, package `dev.ljesus.battmoji`) handles information. It is operated by [COMPANY]. Questions: [CONTACT_EMAIL].

The short version

BattMoji itself does not collect, send, or sell any personal data. Your emoji choice and style settings are saved in a small file on your phone — that is all the app ever keeps. The app shows ads, and Google's ad SDK collects standard ad identifiers as described below. That is the whole picture.

What the app stores on your phone

Everything BattMoji remembers lives in a small local settings file on your device:

  • your chosen emoji
  • the last emoji you picked (a "Recent" row, at most 12 entries)
  • whether the readout shows the percentage and the charging line
  • whether the readout is on, and at which prominence (standard or silent)
  • timestamps used only to space out full-screen ads

This file stays on your phone. BattMoji has no servers, no accounts, no cloud sync, and no analytics SDK. Your battery information — level, health, temperature, voltage, current — is read from your phone's own system, shown on your screen, and never transmitted anywhere.

Android device backup: if you have Android's device backup enabled, Android itself may include this small settings file in your Google-account backup. That is an operating-system feature under your control (Settings → System → Backup). We never see or access that backup.

About the persistent notification (in plain words)

BattMoji's product is a small, silent, ongoing notification that shows your chosen emoji, your battery level, and your charging status — and keeps itself up to date.

  • That notification lives in your phone's notification shade. It is app data displayed to you, on your device — it is not data collected about you, and nothing about your battery is transmitted to us or to anyone else.
  • A small foreground service (declared as "special use", subtype `persistent_battery_icon_notification`) keeps the readout live. It does one job: it listens to the system's battery broadcasts and updates that one notification. It performs no network activity of its own, and it reads no other notifications.
  • If your phone restarts, the readout comes back automatically — because you turned it on. Nothing extra is sent or stored at boot.
  • BattMoji does not use the Accessibility permission, does not draw over other apps, and never reads your other notifications.

Advertising

BattMoji is free, and it is funded by ads: a banner at the bottom of the screen and occasional full-screen ads, served by Google through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob).

  • In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA and the UK), the app shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads. If you decline, ads remain non-personalized.
  • Google's ad SDK may collect standard advertising identifiers and ad-interaction data (for example, impressions and clicks) to serve and measure ads, subject to Google's policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads), and manage ad personalization there or through Google's Ads settings page.
  • We never receive your identity, and we do not build profiles. We only see aggregate ad performance through Google's standard reporting, if at all.

Permissions, in plain language

| Permission | Why it exists |

|---|---|

| Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`, Android 13+) | Shows the battery readout. Asked once, in plain words, when you tap Apply. Optional — without it the readout can't appear; the app tells you so and links you to the right setting. |

| Run at startup (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`) | Brings your readout back after a phone restart, only if you had turned it on. |

| Foreground service (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE` + special use) | Keeps the one readout notification updated live. |

| Internet & network state (`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`) | Loading ads. |

| Advertising ID (`com.google.android.gms.ads.AD_ID`) | Lets the ad SDK work as described above. |

BattMoji does not request: Accessibility, location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, or phone identity.

Children

BattMoji is a general-audience phone personalization utility. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Google's ad serving includes child-directed protections on the ad side, and consent controls apply where required.

Your choices

  • Turn the readout on or off, or reset everything, from the app's Settings tab. Reset clears your emoji, recents, and style choices, and removes the notification.
  • Change prominence (standard or silent) in Settings — or hide the notification any time from the shade itself (its Hide action) or Android's notification settings.
  • Advertising identifiers are controlled in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads, or through the consent form.
  • Uninstalling BattMoji deletes everything the app stored on your phone. There is nothing of yours on our side to clean up.
