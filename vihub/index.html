GTA VI Hub — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-21_

This policy explains, in plain English, what data the GTA VI Hub Android app

(the "App") handles and what it never touches. Short version: the App itself

collects nothing about you. Its guide content is bundled inside the app; the

news headlines are fetched from public gaming-news feeds only when you

refresh, and cached on your device. Advertising is the only other thing that

touches the network, and it runs through Google's ad SDK after you make a

consent choice.

What the App itself collects

Nothing. GTA VI Hub has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics of its

own. The only things stored on your device are:

  • the cached news snapshot (headlines, source labels, links, timestamps) from

your last successful refresh,

  • the fact that you acknowledged the first-launch disclaimer dialog, and
  • advertising frequency-cap bookkeeping.

All of this lives in the App's local storage on your device (Android

SharedPreferences). This data:

  • never leaves your device,
  • is never sent to us or to any third party,
  • is not backed up to any cloud service by us, and
  • is removed when you uninstall the App.

The App's reference content (cheat codes, guides, launch facts) is bundled

inside the App and needs no network at all. Only the news tab uses the

network, and it only downloads public headlines — it never transmits

anything about you.

About the App's name and content (nominative use)

GTA VI Hub is an unofficial, fan-made reference app. It is not affiliated

with, sponsored by or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

"Grand Theft Auto", "GTA" and all related names and marks are trademarks of

their respective owners; the App uses game-related terms descriptively to

identify the subject of its text content, and nothing more. The App contains

no game artwork, screenshots, logos or maps — every fact it shows is text

compiled from publicly available information, with sources cited inside the

App.

Advertising

GTA VI Hub is free and paid for by ads. To show ads, the App embeds the Google

Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). If you use the App where consent is required,

Google's User Messaging Platform shows you a consent form first; your choice

controls whether ads are personalized.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use data as described in Google's

own policies, including:

  • the Advertising ID (and related device identifiers) of your device,
  • limited device and connection information (e.g. network state), and
  • diagnostics (e.g. that an ad request failed or succeeded).

Its standard purposes are to serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud and

abuse. These identifiers and signals are handled by Google, not by us. We do

not receive them, do not build profiles from them, and cannot identify you

with them.

Google's practices are described in:

  • "Privacy & security" / Ads sections of https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • the Google Mobile Ads SDK developer documentation.

No third party other than Google (for advertising) receives any data through

the App.

Analytics

Today the App collects no analytics at all. The App's code contains a

Firebase-based analytics layer that ships switched off — until it is turned

on, nothing in this section happens. When that layer is active, the App

collects anonymized usage events (for example, that you opened a guide

entry, and events about ad requests) together with an app-instance

identifier. That data is processed by Google (Firebase Analytics) under

Google's privacy terms (https://policies.google.com/privacy). It involves

no names, no contact details and no user content — the App has no accounts,

so there is nothing like that to collect.

Data retention

  • Your App data (news cache, acknowledgment flag): kept on your device

only, until you uninstall the App.

  • Advertising-related data held by Google (e.g. the Advertising ID and ad

interaction records): governed by Google's policies and retention terms.

Your controls

  • Ad personalization and the Advertising ID: manage or reset it in

Google's Ads settings at https://adssettings.google.com — you can turn ad

personalization off or reset the identifier at any time.

  • Consent: the consent form Google shows inside the App controls whether

you see personalized ads.

  • Everything: uninstalling GTA VI Hub removes all local App data.

Children

GTA VI Hub is a general-audience reference app and is not directed at

children under 13, and Google's ad SDK is configured accordingly. We do not

knowingly collect any data from anyone.
