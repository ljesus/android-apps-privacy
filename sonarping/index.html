SonarPing — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-20_

This policy explains, in plain English, what the SonarPing Android app

(the "App") does with data. Short version: the App never stores, records

or transmits audio of any kind, everything you set stays on your device,

and the only thing that touches the network is Google's ad SDK — after

you make a consent choice.

The microphone — what actually happens

This is the heart of the App, so here is the complete, honest truth:

**SonarPing uses the microphone only while you have armed the listener or

are running calibration. Audio is processed as a live in-memory loudness

envelope, never written to disk or transmitted.**

In practice:

  • The App reads sound as short windows (~50 milliseconds) and reduces

each one to two numbers: how loud it is, and how much energy sits in

the whistle tone band. Each window is discarded the moment it is

measured — roughly twenty times per second.

  • Nothing is ever recorded. There is no audio file, no buffer saved

for later, no "keep the last N seconds" — nothing.

  • Nothing is ever stored. No audio of any kind is written to your

device's storage.

  • Nothing is ever transmitted. No audio leaves your device, to us or

to anyone.

  • Microphone access is requested only when you arm the listener or start

calibration — never at app open — with an explainer stating exactly

this. The permission is also revocable at any time in your device's

Settings; the App tells you plainly when it is off and never works

around it.

There is nothing to leak, nothing to subpoena and nothing to delete,

because there is nothing kept. Apps in this category that record or

share audio cannot honestly say this; SonarPing can, because the code

doesn't do it. The sound-level envelope exists only in memory, only

while listening, and is discarded per window.

What the App stores on your device

SonarPing keeps a few small settings in the App's local storage (Android

SharedPreferences), all on your device:

  • your calibration (noise floor and trigger thresholds) and chosen

sensitivity preset,

  • your alert preferences (sound, silent-mode behavior, flash, vibration,

alert duration),

  • battery-aware listening preferences,
  • the last ten alert events (time and type — clap or whistle),
  • one-time flags (whether you've seen the intro or a permission

explainer).

This data:

  • never leaves your device,
  • is never sent to us or to any third party,
  • is not backed up to any cloud service by us, and
  • is removed when you uninstall the App, or at once via

Settings → Reset calibration & history.

The App has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics of its own.

Advertising

SonarPing is free and paid for by ads. To show ads, the App embeds the

Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). If you use the App where consent is

required, Google's User Messaging Platform shows you a consent form

first; your choice controls whether ads are personalized.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use data as described in

Google's own policies, including:

  • the Advertising ID (and related device identifiers) of your device,
  • limited device and connection information (e.g. network state), and
  • diagnostics (e.g. that an ad request failed or succeeded).

Its standard purposes are to serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud

and abuse. These identifiers and signals are handled by Google, not by

us. We do not receive them, do not build profiles from them, and cannot

identify you with them. Ad audio or microphone data is never part of

this — the ad SDK has no access to the sound-level envelope, which never

leaves memory in any form.

Google's practices are described in:

  • "Privacy & security" / Ads sections of

https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

  • the Google Mobile Ads SDK developer documentation.

No third party other than Google (for advertising) receives any data

through the App.

Data retention

  • Audio: never retained at all — measured in memory, discarded per

window (~20 times per second) while listening.

  • Your App data (calibration, settings, last ten alerts): kept on

your device only, until you delete it (in-App reset) or uninstall the

App.

  • Advertising-related data held by Google (e.g. the Advertising ID

and ad interaction records): governed by Google's policies and

retention terms.

Your controls

  • Microphone: revoke access any time in your device's Settings →

Apps → SonarPing → Permissions. The App states plainly when the

microphone is off and never arms without it.

  • Ad personalization and the Advertising ID: manage or reset it in

Google's Ads settings at https://adssettings.google.com — you can turn

ad personalization off or reset the identifier at any time.

  • Consent: the consent form Google shows inside the App controls

whether you see personalized ads.

  • App data: Settings → Reset calibration & history clears your

calibration, settings and alert history immediately, after a

confirmation.

  • Everything: uninstalling SonarPing removes all local App data.

Children

SonarPing is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children

under 13, and Google's ad SDK is configured accordingly. We do not

knowingly collect any data from anyone.
