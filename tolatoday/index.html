The short version, first

TolaToday collects no personal data. None. There is no account, no sign-up, and

nothing to sign up with. The app fetches publicly available market rates over the

internet (gold and silver spot from gold-api.com; the USD→INR reference rate

from Frankfurter's European Central Bank feed, with open.er-api.com as an

automatic fallback) and caches those numbers on your device so they survive

offline. Advertising shown by the app goes through Google AdMob and its consent

flow. That is the entire network and data story of this app.

We wrote that paragraph first on purpose. It is the most important thing on this

page.

1. What TolaToday stores, and where

Everything the app stores lives in the app's private storage on your device:

  • The current rates snapshot — gold spot price, silver spot price (when

available), the USD→INR reference rate, which FX feed produced it, the

reference-rate date, and the time it was fetched. Public market numbers only;

nothing about you.

  • Day-change history — up to 30 days of per-day closing values for the

day-change line, recorded on your device. Again, market numbers only.

  • Your settings — your preferred display unit and the auto-refresh toggle.

There is no name, no email, no phone number, no location, no contacts, no files,

no inputs from you — the calculator's numbers you type never leave the screen

they were typed on.

2. Network use — three public market endpoints, nothing else of ours

TolaToday makes plain HTTPS requests to:

  • `api.gold-api.com` (gold and silver spot prices — no key, no account, no

identifying information sent),

  • `api.frankfurter.dev` / `api.frankfurter.app` (ECB daily USD→INR reference

rate),

  • `open.er-api.com` (FX fallback, used only if the ECB feed fails).

These requests carry no personal data — there is none to send. The app contains

no analytics of its own, no crash-reporting SDK beyond what the ad SDK requires,

and no server operated by us: we have nothing to receive.

3. Advertising — the other network use

TolaToday is free, funded by ads served by Google AdMob, presented through

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent flow. Through the ad SDK:

  • Device advertising identifiers and standard ad-interaction data (such as ad

views) may be processed by Google for ad delivery and frequency capping.

  • Ads may be personalized only if you consent in the in-app consent form;

otherwise you see non-personalized ads. You can change or withdraw consent at

any time, and you can reset or delete the advertising identifier in your

Android privacy settings.

  • Ad placement is deliberately conservative: a small banner on the Today screen

only, and an occasional interstitial after you press Calculate — never on app

open.

We collect no other data and run no other tracking of any kind.

4. Android device backups

Android's device backup is enabled for this app (`allowBackup=true`). What that

can ever contain is the cached public market rates, your day-change history, and

your unit/refresh settings — all of it described in section 1. There is no

personal data anywhere in the app, so there is no personal data in any backup.

5. Permissions

The app's manifest requests only `INTERNET` (to fetch rates and serve ads),

`ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` (to know whether a fetch can be attempted), and the

advertising identifier permission used by the ad SDK. TolaToday asks for **no

runtime permissions** — no location, no storage, no contacts, no camera, no

notifications. Nothing is denied to you; nothing is asked of you.

6. Rates are estimates — and this app is not financial advice

Every rate shown is a transparent estimate derived from public market data, not

an official or city-specific rate, and it can differ from what your jeweller

quotes (city, brand and association premiums vary). TolaToday helps you check

and do arithmetic; it offers no buying, selling, lending, or investment feature

of any kind, and makes no price predictions.

7. Children

TolaToday is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13.

The ad SDK is configured accordingly.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and the in-app link

before the change takes effect. Continued use after an update means you accept

the revised policy.
