LiteLeaf — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-20_

This policy explains, in plain English, what data the LiteLeaf Android app

(the "App") handles and what it never touches. Short version: the App itself

collects nothing about you. Your PDF files are rendered on your device and

stay on your device. Advertising is the only thing that touches the network,

and it runs through Google's ad SDK after you make a consent choice.

What the App itself collects

Nothing. LiteLeaf stores your recent-documents list (file titles, page

counts, last-read pages and timestamps), your page bookmarks and your

reading preferences (default background, keep-screen-on) in the App's local

storage on your device (Android SharedPreferences). This data:

  • never leaves your device,
  • is never sent to us or to any third party,
  • is not backed up to any cloud service by us, and
  • is removed when you uninstall the App, or at once via

Settings → Reset all data.

The App has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics of its own.

PDFs you open — including files handed over by other apps

LiteLeaf is a viewer, not a storage service:

  • When another app (a messenger such as WhatsApp, your email app, a browser

or the system Files app) hands LiteLeaf a PDF to display, LiteLeaf receives

only a temporary, read-only access grant for that one file. It renders the

file on your device and never uploads, forwards or shares it.

  • When you pick a PDF yourself, LiteLeaf uses Android's secure file picker

(Storage Access Framework) — the App never asks for access to your phone's

whole storage, and no permission is requested to read the documents you

choose.

  • In the rare case a document source gives LiteLeaf a stream it can't page

through, the App makes a short-lived temporary working copy **in its own

private cache folder** to render from, and deletes that copy the moment

you close the document.

  • LiteLeaf works fully offline. Your files never leave this phone.

Advertising

LiteLeaf is free and paid for by ads. To show ads, the App embeds the

Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). If you use the App where consent is required,

Google's User Messaging Platform shows you a consent form first; your choice

controls whether ads are personalized. Ad placement inside the App is

deliberately limited: a small banner appears on the Library screen only —

never over a document you're reading — and a full-screen ad can appear only

right after you open a new document.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use data as described in Google's

own policies, including:

  • the Advertising ID (and related device identifiers) of your device,
  • limited device and connection information (e.g. network state), and
  • diagnostics (e.g. that an ad request failed or succeeded).

Its standard purposes are to serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud and

abuse. These identifiers and signals are handled by Google, not by us. We do

not receive them, do not build profiles from them, and cannot identify you

with them.

Google's practices are described in:

  • "Privacy & security" / Ads sections of

https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

  • the Google Mobile Ads SDK developer documentation.

No third party other than Google (for advertising) receives any data through

the App.

Data retention

  • Your App data (recents, reading positions, bookmarks, preferences):

kept on your device only, until you delete it (in-App reset) or uninstall

the App. The App never deletes or modifies your actual PDF files.

  • Advertising-related data held by Google (e.g. the Advertising ID and

ad interaction records): governed by Google's policies and retention

terms.

Your controls

  • Ad personalization and the Advertising ID: manage or reset it in

Google's Ads settings at https://adssettings.google.com — you can turn ad

personalization off or reset the identifier at any time.

  • Consent: the consent form Google shows inside the App controls whether

you see personalized ads.

  • App data: Settings → Reset all data clears your recents, saved

positions and bookmarks immediately, after a confirmation. Your actual

files on the phone are not touched.

  • Everything: uninstalling LiteLeaf removes all local App data.

Children

LiteLeaf is a general-audience document utility and is not directed at

children under 13, and Google's ad SDK is configured accordingly. We do not

knowingly collect any data from anyone.
