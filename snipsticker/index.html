SnipSticker — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-20_

This policy explains, in plain English, what data the SnipSticker Android

app (the "App", package `dev.ljesus.snipsticker`) handles and what it never

touches. Short version: the App itself collects nothing about you. Photos

and stickers stay on your device; advertising is the only thing that touches

the network, and it runs through Google's ad SDK after you make a consent

choice.

What the App itself collects

Nothing. SnipSticker has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics of

its own.

Photos and stickers — on your device only

  • The photos you pick are processed entirely on your device: position,

cut-out brush, optional outline and WebP encoding all run in the App.

Photos are never uploaded and never sent to us or to any third party.

  • Finished stickers and packs are stored in the **App's private internal

storage** on your device. No other app can browse them.

  • A sticker mid-cut is held in memory only. It is not written to storage

and is discarded if the App closes before you save it to a pack — the

App's Settings screen says exactly this ("Unsaved work").

  • Reset all data (Settings → Danger zone) deletes every pack and

sticker at once, after a confirmation. Uninstalling the App removes

everything as well.

What WhatsApp gets — only when you tap Add

When you tap Add to WhatsApp, you hand that one pack's files to

WhatsApp on your device, using WhatsApp's own official sticker-pack flow —

WhatsApp shows you its own confirmation screen before anything happens.

After you add a pack, WhatsApp keeps its own copy of it; from that

point WhatsApp's own policies govern that copy. SnipSticker sends nothing

to WhatsApp without that explicit tap, and there is no other network

traffic in the sticker flow.

Advertising

SnipSticker is free and paid for by ads. To show ads, the App embeds the

Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Where consent is required, Google's User

Messaging Platform shows you a consent form first; your choice controls

whether ads are personalized.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use data as described in

Google's own policies, including:

  • the Advertising ID (and related device identifiers) of your device,
  • limited device and connection information (e.g. network state), and
  • diagnostics (e.g. that an ad request failed or succeeded).

Its standard purposes are to serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud

and abuse. These identifiers and signals are handled by Google, not by us.

We do not receive them, do not build profiles from them, and cannot identify

you with them.

Google's practices are described at

https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and in the Google Mobile Ads

SDK developer documentation.

No third party other than Google (for advertising) receives any data

through the App.

Analytics

Today the App collects no analytics at all. The App's code contains a

Firebase-based analytics layer that ships switched off — until it is turned

on, nothing in this section happens. When that layer is active, the App

collects anonymized usage events (for example, that you added a sticker

pack to WhatsApp, and events about ad requests) together with an

app-instance identifier. That data is processed by Google (Firebase

Analytics) under Google's privacy terms (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

It involves no names, no contact details and no user content — your photos

and sticker files never enter analytics; they stay on your device as

described above. The App has no accounts, so there is nothing like that to

collect.

Data retention

  • Your App data (packs, stickers, tray icons): kept on your device

only, until you delete it via Reset all data or uninstall the App.

  • Unsaved work (a sticker mid-cut): memory only — gone when the App

closes.

  • Advertising-related data held by Google (e.g. the Advertising ID and

ad-interaction records): governed by Google's policies and retention

terms.

Your controls

  • Ad personalization / Advertising ID: manage or reset it in Google's

Ads settings at https://adssettings.google.com — you can turn ad

personalization off or reset the identifier at any time.

  • Consent: the form Google shows inside the App controls whether you

see personalized ads; you can revisit the choice in your device's ad

privacy settings.

  • App data: Settings → Reset all data clears every pack and

sticker immediately, after a confirmation.

  • Everything: uninstalling SnipSticker removes all local App data.

Children

SnipSticker is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children

under 13, and Google's ad SDK is configured accordingly. We do not

knowingly collect any data from anyone.
