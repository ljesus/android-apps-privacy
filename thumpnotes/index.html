Thump Notes — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-22_

This policy explains, in plain English, what data the Thump Notes Android app

(the "App") handles and what it never touches. Short version: the App itself

collects nothing about you. Its reference content is bundled inside the App;

the news headlines are fetched from four public Indian automotive feeds only

when you refresh, and cached on your device. Advertising is the only other

thing that touches the network, and it runs through Google's ad SDK after you

make a consent choice.

What the App itself collects

Nothing. Thump Notes has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics of its

own. The only things stored on your device are:

  • the cached news snapshot (headlines, source labels, links, timestamps) from

your last successful refresh,

  • the fact that you acknowledged the first-launch disclaimer dialog, and
  • advertising frequency-cap bookkeeping.

All of this lives in the App's local storage on your device (Android

SharedPreferences). This data:

  • never leaves your device,
  • is never sent to us or to any third party,
  • is not backed up to any cloud service by us, and
  • is removed when you uninstall the App.

The App's reference content (the 250cc tracker, lineup rows, ownership notes)

is bundled inside the App and needs no network at all. Only the News tab uses

the network, and it only fetches public headlines — the requests carry no

user data, and nothing about you is ever transmitted. Tapping a headline

opens it in your browser app; the App never renders third-party pages itself.

About the App's name and content (nominative use)

Thump Notes is an unofficial, fan-made news and reference app. It is not

affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Royal Enfield or Eicher Motors.

"Royal Enfield" and model names are trademarks of Eicher Motors Ltd.; the App

uses them descriptively to identify the subject of its text content, and

nothing more. The App contains no Royal Enfield artwork, logos, photos or

bike imagery — every fact it shows is text compiled from publicly available

information, with each claim's source and date shown beside it.

Advertising

Thump Notes is free and paid for by ads. To show ads, the App embeds the

Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). If you use the App where consent is required,

Google's User Messaging Platform shows you a consent form first; your choice

controls whether ads are personalized.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use data as described in Google's

own policies, including:

  • the Advertising ID (and related device identifiers) of your device,
  • limited device and connection information (e.g. network state), and
  • diagnostics (e.g. that an ad request failed or succeeded).

Its standard purposes are to serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud and

abuse. These identifiers and signals are handled by Google, not by us. We do

not receive them, do not build profiles from them, and cannot identify you

with them.

Google's practices are described in:

  • "Privacy & security" / Ads sections of https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • the Google Mobile Ads SDK developer documentation.

No third party other than Google (for advertising) receives any data through

the App. The four news feeds (gaadiwaadi.com, rushlane.com, motorbeam.com,

autocarpro.in) receive anonymous one-off feed requests, like any RSS reader

— with no user data attached.

Analytics

Today the App collects no analytics at all. The App's code contains a

Firebase-based analytics layer that ships switched off — until it is turned

on, nothing in this section happens. When that layer is active, the App

collects anonymized usage events (for example, that a reference entry was

opened, and events about ad requests) together with an app-instance

identifier. That data is processed by Google (Firebase Analytics) under

Google's privacy terms (https://policies.google.com/privacy). It involves no

names, no contact details and no user content — the App has no accounts, so

there is nothing like that to collect.

Data retention

  • Your App data (news cache, acknowledgment flag): kept on your device

only, until you uninstall the App.

  • Advertising-related data held by Google (e.g. the Advertising ID and ad

interaction records): governed by Google's policies and retention terms.

Your controls

  • Ad personalization and the Advertising ID: manage or reset it in

Google's Ads settings at https://adssettings.google.com — you can turn ad

personalization off or reset the identifier at any time.

  • Consent: the consent form Google shows inside the App controls whether

you see personalized ads.

  • Everything: uninstalling Thump Notes removes all local App data.

Children

Thump Notes is a general-audience news and reference app and is not directed

at children under 13, and Google's ad SDK is configured accordingly. We do

not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
