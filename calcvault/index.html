The short version, first

Your photos and videos are stored encrypted on this device (AES-256-GCM).

The vault key is unwrapped only with your PIN and never leaves your phone's

hardware-backed keystore. CalcVault works fully offline — the only network use

is advertising. Without your PIN and recovery answer, your files cannot be

recovered — by us or anyone.

We wrote that paragraph first on purpose. It is the most important thing on

this page.

1. What CalcVault stores, and where

  • Your photos and videos are imported through Android's system photo

picker and written directly into the app's private storage as

AES-256-GCM-encrypted files with random UUID filenames. **No plaintext copy

is ever written** — encryption happens while the file is being copied, and

thumbnails are stored as encrypted sidecar files too.

  • The vault key is a random 256-bit key. It is stored only in wrapped

form: once under a key derived from your PIN (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with a

random salt and an iteration count benchmarked on your device), and once as

a backup wrap under a hardware-backed Android Keystore key. The backup wrap

exists solely so that your security answer can re-lock the vault under a

new PIN if you forget the old one. It is not a back door and never bypasses

your PIN.

  • Your PIN is never stored anywhere. A wrong PIN simply fails to unwrap

the key, and the app keeps behaving as a calculator.

  • Your security answer is stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash, never in

readable form.

  • Android device backups are switched off (`allowBackup=false`). Your

files and key material are never included in Google's device-to-cloud

backups. There is no copy of your vault anywhere but this phone.

2. Files never leave your device

CalcVault has no upload code, no account system, and no server. Imported

files stay in the app's private storage. Export uses the system file picker

(you choose the destination), and sharing uses a short-lived private cache

copy that is purged. Video playback also uses a short-lived private cache

copy that is purged the moment you leave the viewer or the vault locks.

Because everything is local: **uninstalling CalcVault permanently removes the

app and every remaining file it stores.** In-app deletion is also permanent —

real encryption means there is no recycle bin.

3. The honest limit

If you forget your PIN and your recovery answer, your files cannot be

recovered — by us or anyone. There is no reset we can perform, no copy of

your key on any server, and no way to decrypt your files without the wrapped

key. Apps that promise otherwise are either storing your files unencrypted or

holding a copy of your key. CalcVault does neither.

4. Advertising — the only network use

CalcVault is free, funded by ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its

consent flow (Google's User Messaging Platform) are the only components of

this app that touch the network. Through them:

  • Device advertising identifiers and standard ad-interaction data (e.g. ad

views) may be processed for ad delivery and frequency capping.

  • Ads may be personalized only if you consent through the in-app consent

form; otherwise you see non-personalized ads. You can change or withdraw

consent at any time, and you can reset or delete the advertising

identifier in your Android privacy settings.

  • Ad traffic is transmitted over encrypted connections.

We collect no other data: no name, no email, no analytics of our own, no

usage tracking beyond what the ad SDK needs to show ads.

5. Permissions

CalcVault requests no runtime permissions — no gallery access, no storage

permission, no camera, no location, no contacts. Import happens through

Android's system photo picker; export through the system file picker. The

app never asks for, and never receives, access to your whole gallery.

6. Screen privacy

Whenever you are inside the vault (after unlocking), the app sets the

Android secure-screen flag, so the vault never appears in the recent-apps

thumbnails or in screen captures of other apps. The calculator facade is

the only screen that shows in recents — by design.

7. Children

CalcVault is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under

13 (the ad SDK is configured accordingly).

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and the in-app

policy link before the change takes effect. Continued use after an update

means you accept the revised policy.
