BB20 Insider — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-22_

This policy explains, in plain English, what data the BB20 Insider Android

app (the "App") handles and what it never touches. Short version: the App

itself collects nothing about you. Its season reference content is bundled

inside the app; the news headlines are fetched from public Indian

entertainment feeds only when you refresh, and cached on your device.

Advertising is the only other thing that touches the network, and it runs

through Google's ad SDK after you make a consent choice.

What the App itself collects

Nothing. BB20 Insider has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics of

its own. The only things stored on your device are:

  • the cached news snapshot (headlines, source labels, links, timestamps)

from your last successful refresh,

  • the fact that you acknowledged the first-launch disclaimer dialog, and
  • advertising frequency-cap bookkeeping and Google's consent record.

All of this lives in the App's local storage on your device (Android

SharedPreferences). This data:

  • never leaves your device,
  • is never sent to us or to any third party,
  • is not backed up to any cloud service by us, and
  • is removed when you uninstall the App.

The App's reference content (season status, the eviction timeline, the

format primer, the voting explainer, every winner from seasons 1–19) is

bundled inside the App and needs no network at all. Only the news tab uses

the network, and it only fetches public headlines — it never transmits

anything about you.

About the App's name and content (nominative use)

BB20 Insider is an unofficial, fan-made news and reference app. It is

not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Colors TV, Viacom18,

JioStar or the Bigg Boss programme. "Bigg Boss" and all related names and

marks are trademarks of their respective owners; the App uses show-related

terms descriptively to identify the subject of its text content, and

nothing more. The App contains no show artwork, stills, logos or photos —

every fact it shows is text compiled from publicly available news

coverage, with the outlets named inside the entries. The App hosts no

voting and links no voting pages; it simply explains how the real audience

vote works.

Advertising

BB20 Insider is free and paid for by ads. To show ads, the App embeds the

Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). If you use the App where consent is

required, Google's User Messaging Platform shows you a consent form

first; your choice controls whether ads are personalized.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use data as described in

Google's own policies, including:

  • the Advertising ID (and related device identifiers) of your device,
  • limited device and connection information (e.g. network state), and
  • diagnostics (e.g. that an ad request failed or succeeded).

Its standard purposes are to serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud

and abuse. These identifiers and signals are handled by Google, not by

us. We do not receive them, do not build profiles from them, and cannot

identify you with them.

Google's practices are described in:

  • "Privacy & security" / Ads sections of https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • the Google Mobile Ads SDK developer documentation.

No third party other than Google (for advertising) receives any data

through the App.

Analytics

Today the App collects no analytics at all. The App's code contains a

Firebase-based analytics layer that ships switched off — until it is

turned on, nothing in this section happens. When that layer is active, the

App collects anonymized usage events (for example, that you opened a

season-reference entry, and events about ad requests) together with an

app-instance identifier. That data is processed by Google (Firebase

Analytics) under Google's privacy terms

(https://policies.google.com/privacy). It involves no names, no contact

details and no user content — the App has no accounts, so there is nothing

like that to collect.

Data retention

  • Your App data (news cache, acknowledgment flag, consent record):

kept on your device only, until you uninstall the App.

  • Advertising-related data held by Google (e.g. the Advertising ID and

ad interaction records): governed by Google's policies and retention

terms.

Your controls

  • Ad personalization and the Advertising ID: manage or reset it in

Google's Ads settings at https://adssettings.google.com — you can turn

ad personalization off or reset the identifier at any time.

  • Consent: the consent form Google shows inside the App controls

whether you see personalized ads.

  • Everything: uninstalling BB20 Insider removes all local App data.

Children

BB20 Insider is a general-audience news and reference app and is not

directed at children under 13, and Google's ad SDK is configured

accordingly. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
